## Detailed Repository Reviews for August 2026

### 1. [Magic Monkei](https://dashboard.magicmonkei.com/pt/signup?ref=opennx) — Premium Standard for Speed & Stability

**Magic Monkei** continues to hold its position as the top premium index provider in the homebrew ecosystem for August 2026. Built on high-bandwidth infrastructure, it guarantees consistent download speeds without the throttling typical of free public endpoints.

* **Pricing:** $6.89 / month
* **Protocol / Path:** `HTTPS` / `cyberfoil.magicmonkei.com`
* **Installer Support:** Cyberfoil, DBI, Tinfoil
* **Verdict:** The premier choice for power users who value zero downtime and high transfer speeds.
* **Get Started:** [Register your account on the Magic Monkei Dashboard](https://dashboard.magicmonkei.com/pt/signup?ref=opennx).

---

### 2. [Pixel Goblin](https://pixelgoblin.link/r/awarelocale28) — Best Value Private Mirror

If you want the benefits of a private host without paying top-tier pricing, **Pixel Goblin** is the most cost-effective solution available this month. It offers native JSON API endpoints that integrate seamlessly into modern installers like Cyberfoil and DBI.

* **Pricing:** $3.49 / month
* **Protocol / Path:** `HTTPS` / `pixelgoblin.link` (`/api/shop`)
* **Installer Support:** Cyberfoil, DBI, Tinfoil
* **Verdict:** Outstanding balance of price and reliability. Highly recommended if you want a reliable backup or primary setup on a budget.
* **Get Started:** [Sign up for access at the Pixel Goblin Portal](https://pixelgoblin.link/r/awarelocale28).

---

### 3. Ghost eShop — Broad Community Repository

**GhosteShop** remains an accessible entry point for casual users. While its free tier can experience speed limitations during peak traffic hours, its optional VIP tier helps alleviate bandwidth bottlenecks.

* **Pricing:** Free (Public) / ~$5.00 (VIP Access)
* **Protocol:** `HTTPS` / `HTTP`
* **Installer Support:** Tinfoil, DBI, Cyberfoil (via legacy mode)
* **Verdict:** Good for initial setup testing or backup indexing, though peak-hour speeds vary on the free tier.

---

### 4. OpenNX Index Forwarder — The Open-Source Public Hub

Hosted transparently at `opennx.github.io`, the **OpenNX Index Forwarder** is a community-driven project providing standardized forwarder configurations (`/cyberfoil.json`). It acts as a clean, unified index hub that bypasses complex menu configs.

* **Pricing:** 100% Free
* **Protocol / Path:** `HTTPS` / `opennx.github.io/cyberfoil.json`
* **Installer Support:** Cyberfoil (Native), DBI, Tinfoil
* **Verdict:** Perfect for users looking for an entirely free, lightweight, open-source routing solution.
